Why volume matters

One inbox can't do this. A hundred can.

ONE MAILBOX

About 10 safe emails a day. Push harder and you land in spam. 10 a day × 22 send days is about 200 people a month. At 1 interested reply per 250 to 300 emails, that's maybe one interested person a month.

100 MAILBOXES

Every mailbox is like one rep sending 10 safe emails a day. 100 mailboxes is 1,000 emails a day, about 22,000 a month. Same safe sending, just more mailboxes. Want double? Double the mailboxes.
